# Contributing

Contribution guidelines are centralized for the whole Ichava org:

- [Contributing guide](https://github.com/ichava/documentation/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/ichava/documentation/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

For this pack specifically: icon assets are refreshed from upstream by the
[maintainer-toolkit sync](https://github.com/ichava/documentation/blob/main/icon-pack-maintainer-sync.md).
Please do not hand-edit generated SVGs; contribute fixes to the sync recipe
or the upstream source instead.
